Figure 8. Prescribed MCDA weights compared with grouped XGBoost SHAP attribution for the 13 conditioning factors. (a) Normalised AHP, FAHP and ANP weights (each set sums to 1). (b) Grouped mean absolute SHAP share (%) of the full-data XGBoost explanatory model, from 20,000 explanation rows on the raw-margin (log-odds) scale, with encoded inputs summed to the 13 factors. (c) Rank of each factor under the four sets of values (1 = largest); the grey bar spans the lowest to the highest rank. Factors are ordered by SHAP rank. MCDA weights are analyst-prescribed input priorities; SHAP shares are model-specific, associational attributions of a fitted model that was not one of the strict event- and geography-withheld evaluation models. The two quantities are different, are shown on separate axes, and are compared by ordering only; neither represents a causal effect.
