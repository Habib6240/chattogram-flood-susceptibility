Figure S1. The 13 final conditioning factors on the common 30 m analysis domain (EPSG:32646; 4,722,216 valid cells, 4,249.99 km²). Panels a–m follow the order of Table S3; panel n gives the categorical legends and panel o the north arrow, a 10 km scale bar and display notes. Every panel shows the same valid domain: cells outside the domain are white, and the thin black line is the area-of-interest boundary. Continuous factors use a 2nd–98th percentile display stretch calculated on the valid domain, with values beyond the stretch taking the end colours; this stretch is for display and differs from the 5th–95th percentile bounds used for MCDA standardization (Table S1). Drainage density and the two curvature factors are shown on a relative Low–High scale because their source units are not recorded in the audited package, and the curvature scale is symmetric about zero. Aspect is shown over 0–360° with flat terrain as a separate grey class. LULC (ESA WorldCover 2021) and HSG are categorical. Rainfall (about 0.05°) and HSG (about 250 m) have coarser native support than the common grid (Table S1). The panels document model inputs and show no results; and the layers are not matched in time to the flood events (temporal bases are listed in Table S1).
